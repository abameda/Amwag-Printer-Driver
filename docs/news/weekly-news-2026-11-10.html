What’s in this update

Microsoft-certified Kyocera driver

Kyocera KX v4 driver 6.2.2215, signed by Microsoft, so Windows installs it without extra warnings.

Inside the package, no download

The driver ships with the tool. Size, SHA-256 and signature are checked before every install.

Ticket settings applied for you

AMWAG Ticket 3.27 × 6.70 in from the MP tray: portrait, single-sided, 100 % scale.

Older drivers upgraded automatically

PCs on the old Kyocera KX v3 driver are switched to v4, which accepts the ticket size.
